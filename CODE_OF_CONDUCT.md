# Code of Conduct

Huxley adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to **conduct@usehuxley.io**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers, who may take any action they consider appropriate, up to and including a permanent ban, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
